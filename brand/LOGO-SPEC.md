## Construction (cap height = 100)
- Stroke 14. Widths K 74 · U 76 · S 70 · P 68. Spacing K–U 40 · U–S 46 · S–P 48.
- K: stem + detached chevron, vertex y = 54, registration gap 6.4 (0.46 × stroke).
- Datum: 6-unit rule at y = 118, full word width; benchmark tick 6 × 24 at origin; registration tick 3 × 15 at end.
- Descriptor: Hanken Grotesk 600 outlined, cap 15, tracking 0.16 em, two lines, 1.5-unit rule 40 units from the word.

## Optical QA result
- Spacing: mean white-area gaps K–U 61.1 · U–S 63.1 · S–P 61.9 units — even; no change.
- K chevron: reads clearly detached at ≥ 48 px. At 16 / 24 / 32 px the detached K broke up ("I(" effect), so the favicon uses a connected K (stroke 26).
- Datum: 5 → 6 units (and ticks 3 / 6 wide) so it survives at 96–120 px wordmark widths; registration gap 5 → 6.4.
- Gold datum on near-black stays subordinate (thin rule, no fill weight).
- Descriptor legible from ~240 px, comfortable at 280 px (cap 5.4 px). Not enlarged.
