KUSP / Identity / For approval — not deployed

Logo system

Every KUSP capital is constructed from geometry on a 100-unit cap-height grid — not typeset. The descriptor uses Hanken Grotesk 600, converted to outlines. All files are self-contained SVG in brand/svg/.

01 / Three directions

Concept directions

A — Datum RECOMMENDED

Constructed architectural capitals at a 14-unit stroke with optical spacing (40 / 46 / 48). The K's arm is held off its stem by a small registration gap — the single identity detail. A burgundy datum carries the word, with a benchmark tick at its origin and a registration tick at its end: the language of survey, section and planning sheet. Calm at any size; reads as institutional, not tech.

B — Registration

Lighter constructed capitals (8.5 stroke, wider tracking) paired with a printer’s / survey registration mark holding a burgundy decision square. Precise and technical, but the symbol is a familiar device and pulls the identity toward “engineering firm”; at small sizes it competes with the word.

C — Parcel grid

KUSP arranged as four parcels on a plan, with street centrelines in the gutters and one burgundy “decision parcel”. Strong as a square avatar, but it is a monogram block rather than a wordmark, reads less clearly as a name, and edges toward decorative.

04 / Usage

Clear space & minimum size

Clear space

Keep a clear zone of ½ H (half the cap height of KUSP) on all sides of every lockup. For the compact mark, the tile edge is the boundary; keep ⅛ of the tile width clear around it.
