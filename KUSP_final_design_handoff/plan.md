# KUSP — Full Website Design Approval Plan

## Scope and decision boundary

This phase creates **eight visual approval prototypes only** for KUSP — Krishvi Unified Solutions Partners. No production architecture, framework migration, public deployment, live form handling, data store, or Claude handoff is in scope. The approved Home page remains the Living Masterplan foundation and may receive alignment QA only; it is not redesigned.

The Corporate Capabilities PDF is the content source of truth. Every page excludes unsupported projects, clients, locations, awards, statistics, contact details, offices, social links, named team members, portraits, biographies, initials, historic employers, and public people metadata. Diagrams are schematic and unattributed; they never imply client work, real geography, live data, performance results, or a commissioned project.

## Shared visual system

> **One planning-exhibition world; eight non-repeating page experiences.**

KUSP uses warm ivory drawing fields, near-black pauses, burgundy decision marks, deep-burgundy active states, architectural-gold traces, muted landscape green, water blue, white, thin architectural rules, square geometry, asymmetric 12-column compositions, and strong whitespace. Libre Caslon Text is reserved for selected editorial declarations; Hanken Grotesk handles body copy, labels, controls, and detailed capability content. No cards, rounded SaaS UI, shadow-led surfaces, glow, decorative gradients, neon, floating panels, particles, dashboard patterns, generic consultancy layout, people imagery, or repeated masterplan framing.

All pages share a solid adaptive sticky header with a protected clearance band. The header changes between ivory and near-black sections using an observed section tone, never overlaps a section code or headline, and retains visible focus. At desktop, common outer margins and baselines govern index, heading, explanatory copy, visual stage, and controls. At mobile, every page uses 24px outer margins, readable line lengths, 44px control targets, stable 2D diagrams, no accidental horizontal overflow, and reduced-motion static states.

## Content hierarchy and interaction direction

### Home — final QA only

The approved Home retains its H1, integrated narrative, five practices, framework, sectors, six differentiators, non-personal experience wall, Insights placeholder, and resolved CTA. QA protects the separate hero editorial field, readable adaptive header, sector typology stage, six-axis Why KUSP field, neutral evidence fragments, and high aerial closure.

### About — Spatial Manifesto / Layered Practice

The page uses verified Who We Are, Mission & Design Philosophy, Global & Local, Physical & Digital, Design & Impact, Multidisciplinary Expertise, Why KUSP, and a restrained source-supported invitation. Its hero is a front-on, vertical exploded practice section: contextual, spatial-intelligence, economic-strategy, environmental-stewardship, and built/digital planes register along one burgundy datum. Three paired manifesto controls change layer emphasis but never hide content. The six differentiators appear as a ruled typographic field; no team content appears.

### Expertise — Five Professional Lenses

A tight, near-top-down abstract site slice presents all PDF-verified capability lists. Five text-led selectors reveal distinct diagram grammars: parcel/massing bands; advisory dependency flow; geospatial registered grid; infrastructure corridor network; and watershed blue-green contours. All capability text remains in semantic document flow. An integrated reset registers the five traces into a neutral boundary.

### Spatial Intelligence Framework™ — Transformation Sequence

The official Observe → Analyse → Integrate → Optimise → Deliver stages form the strongest interactive page. A high flattened planning-board state moves from existing condition to generic GIS/data overlays, joined systems, schematic A/B/Preferred comparison, and a resolved plan. A persistent numbered stage rail supports direct selection and keyboard use. The diagrams remain generic, with no named software, source datasets, project claims, or quantified outcome.

### Sectors — Spatial Typologies

All eight PDF-verified sectors appear as separate selectable micro-worlds: urban parcel field; logistics corridor/cluster grid; multimodal network; utility matrix; civic campus; watershed system; heritage precinct; and data field. They are direct typology states rather than cards. A persistent index and schematic/not-to-scale label make all names and scope statements discoverable without interaction.

### Experience — Planning Studio / Evidence Wall

The page holds nine source-supported thematic groups: statutory planning, master planning, industrial development, regional planning, GIS/spatial analysis, mobility, economic feasibility, policy/advisory, and procurement/implementation. Each is represented by a neutral technical fragment, not a named case study. A selected theme enlarges its sheet and supports a source-safe textual label; the page explicitly describes the artifacts as schematic studies with no project attribution.

### Insights — Editorial Journal

The standalone Insights page is intentionally minimal: Perspective, Research, Thinking, and the approved placeholder, “Insights and research from the practice will appear here as they are published.” There are no articles, authors, dates, publication names, filters, or archive metrics.

### Contact — Calm Resolved Spatial Field

The Contact prototype contains Start a Project, a source-supported invitation, and a single ruled enquiry form with Organisation, Project type, Sector, Location, Project stage, and Message. Inputs are visual demonstrations only; submission produces an honest local prototype confirmation, not a real transmission. No email, telephone, address, office, response-time, privacy/legal, or availability claim is introduced.

## Motion system

Motion follows planning logic only: line drawing, layer registration and reveal, parcel separation, corridor tracing, landscape growth, GIS emergence, scenario comparison, camera reframing, and final resolution. No autoplay dependency, parallax, scroll-jacking, dramatic fly-throughs, bouncy motion, glow, or decorative particles. Every interaction carries labels, text, keyboard/focus support, and reduced-motion completion states.
