| `--pale-line` | `rgba(241,236,223,.24)` | Dark-field drawing rule |

### Typography

- **Display:** `Libre Caslon Text`, Georgia fallback. Use for selected editorial declarations only.
- **System/body/controls:** `Hanken Grotesk`, Arial fallback.
- **H1:** `clamp(3.5rem, 6.5vw, 7.3rem)`; editorial, concise, low line-height.
- **H2:** `clamp(2.7rem, 4.6vw, 5.35rem)`; only selective `em` treatment.
- **Body:** approximately `0.95rem`, `1.58` line-height.
- **Eyebrow / section labels:** `0.58–0.62rem`, uppercase, 0.13–0.16em letter spacing, Hanken Grotesk 700.
- **Controls:** Hanken Grotesk 600–700, small uppercase/indexed labels; never below readable mobile scale.

### Rules for the editorial voice

- Use serif + gold italic only for signature declarations, not every heading.
- Alternate typographic expression: sans-led headings, burgundy non-italic emphasis, green non-italic sector emphasis, and restrained serif sections.
- Headlines are direct, spatial and specific. CTAs use verbs such as **Explore Our Practice**, **Partner With Us**, and **Start a Project**.

### Grid, anchors, and spacing

- Desktop: **12-column asymmetrical grid**, outer margins **4.5vw**, 20px internal gap baseline.
- Mobile: single-column / intentionally stacked layout with fixed **24px** outer margins.
- Sticky header: **82px desktop, 72px mobile**. Section codes must begin below its protected clearance band.
- `section-index`: 115px from top desktop / 98px mobile before local composition offsets; keep codes clear of logo and headline.
- Major sections: 150px top padding desktop, 125px mobile; use generous but purposeful white space.
- Use square geometry, thin rules, borders, plotted offsets, and hard edges—never card shadows or rounded containers.

### Header

- Same structure across all eight pages: KUSP wordmark left; full nav desktop; compact two-line menu mark mobile.
- Header background switches automatically: ivory on light fields and near-black on dark fields.
- Mobile menu control must remain **at least 44 × 44px**. Header controls need visible keyboard focus.
- Desktop nav spacing: 17px. Wordmark placement: 4.5vw desktop / 24px mobile.

## 5. Motion and interaction rules

### Motion language

Motion must explain the planning process: building assembly, parcel separation, corridor tracing, landscape growth, GIS appearance, layer isolation, scenario separation, camera reframing, and final plan resolution.

### Prohibited motion

No parallax dependency, scroll-jacking, long fly-throughs, spins, bouncing, generic looping, glow, particles, loading theatrics, or motion that hides essential content.

### Interaction requirements

- Every selectable state needs a visible text label and keyboard focus state.
- Hover and focus must produce equivalent changes where applicable.
- Active state uses rule/color/diagram transformation—not an opaque card.
- Mobile controls: at least **44 × 44px**. Framework stage rail may scroll horizontally rather than collapse labels.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` must preserve a complete static end state, not remove the plan or interaction information.

---

## 6. Accessibility requirements

- One semantic H1 per page; use sequential H2/H3 relationships.
- Use real `<button>` elements for state changes and real `<a>` links for navigation.
- Maintain visible `:focus-visible` states on controls and nav links.
- Supply `aria-pressed` for selectable controls and update it with state.
- Keep diagrams decorative unless a textual equivalent is available. Do not hide unique content inside images.
- Avoid color-only state: pair color with active rule, underline, label, or positional change.
- Preserve readable contrast on ivory and near-black header states.
- Avoid sensory-only instructions and auto-advance dependencies.
- Maintain 24px mobile gutters and prevent viewport overflow.

---

## 7. Performance requirements

- Preserve CSS/SVG spatial diagrams as lightweight design primitives; do not substitute heavy video, WebGL, stock photography, or repeated raster masterplans.
- Use `font-display: swap` for web fonts.
- Prefer transform/opacity transitions for the limited planning-motion system.
- Avoid layout-shifting hero assets; reserve dimensions for any future artwork.
- Do not create unnecessary client-side data fetching, dashboards, analytics displays, or map SDK dependencies.
- Keep the Contact form local until a verified delivery endpoint and privacy model are approved.
